# Welcome to College Algebra

## Course Scope 

MATH110, College Algebra, is a distance learning course designed to help students achieve a greater understanding of the mathematical concepts and problem solving techniques important for their college work. It covers a variety of mathematical concepts and techniques that are important to those continuing into more advanced math and math-related classes.

The course is organized into four distinct parts.

- The first part of the course covers the basic concepts involved in working with linear and quadratic equations, inequalities, radicals and polynomials.

- The second part of the course investigates the solution and graphing of systems of linear and nonlinear equations and inequalities, as well as power and polynomial functions.

- The third part of the course concentrates on functions, composition and transformation of functions, and the use of exponential and logarithmic expressions.

- The final part of the course considers the conic sections.

Practical applications are provided throughout the course. 

## Course Objectives

CO1: Identify algebraic functions and conic sections.  

CO2: Solve algebraic problems using graphs, equations, and inequalities.  

CO3: Use the rectangular coordinate system to describe a variety of mathematical functions and their characteristics.  

CO4: Discuss algebraic concepts and their characteristics, and how they apply to situations in everyday life.  

CO5: Construct mathematical models to solve application problems.  

CO6: Use algebra to make informed decisions.
