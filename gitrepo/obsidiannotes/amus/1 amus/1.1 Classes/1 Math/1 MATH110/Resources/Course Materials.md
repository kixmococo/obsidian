# Course Materials

Lessons are intended to introduce to you the material in the course, give you practice using the concepts introduced in that material, and assess your ability to do problems involving the material you've been learning and practicing.  **LimeSpring** provides all the lesson content, e.g. videos, for each week of your course.  Embedded in these videos are quick, graded knowledge checks that count as your homework grades.  Before you can do your Workbook practice problems or take the Book exams which are also in **LimeSpring**, you must complete the corresponding videos and knowledge checks.   In addition to the **LimeSpring** videos that present your lesson content, if you have question about a topic covered in a video there are several places you can get help!

1. There are links in pdf files in the “Foundational Knowledge” at the top of your course’s content that take you to the **OpenStax** textbook on algebra.  Just look for the topic you have questions about, click the link to that topic and read more about it in the **OpenStax** textbook.
2. There are cheat sheets and handy helpers for every lesson that offer additional help on all topics.
3. You can use a tutor if you want to, for free!  The link for help from a tutor is at the bottom of every “Additional Resources” page in your course.

 Remember that the first person you should think of if you need help is your Instructor.  You can click on the “Envelope Icon” at the top of your browser page to Instant Message or email your Instructor directly with questions.  In addition, there is a Q&A Discussion in every week of your course, e.g. for the first week of the course this discussion is named “W1: Questions About This Week’s Work?”  That discussion every week is for any questions you have.  If you have a question others probably will too!  So, check there to see if your question has already been posted and answered! In summary, go to **LimeSpring** to do all your work in your course, reading/videos about lesson topics, knowledge checks for homework grades, Workbooks for practice problems, and Book Exams.  The link to **LimeSpring** is in the Activities & Assessments block of every week.   Always remember you are not alone.  There are lots of places to get help any time you need it.  Good Luck!

You will find the link you need to  **LimeSpring** under Activities & Assessments. These lessons are in addition to the Discussions and other course features designed  to help you learn college algebra and master MATH110!  If you ever have any question about any of the materials be sure to ask your instructor!    

## Additional Resources

Your primary resource should be the LimeSpring which have been designed to match the topics we study.

If you feel the need to additional information, each week's **Handy Helpers** link will connect you to a wide variety of resources:

- Internet sites
- the APUS/AMU Library
- a Textbook
- Calculators
- Information on Tutoring

Unlike many online math classes that expect students to just read the textbook and then take tests, our class is a resource-rich learning environment with lectures, practice problems and a wealth of internet Handy Helpers.

They say that it takes a village to raise a child. Well, sometimes it takes more than one source to explain a math concept! Each week supplementary resources are provided to give you some additional perspectives on how to tackle our algebra problems.  There is a wealth of information on-line to help you understand mathematics. The resources listed in the Handy Helpers have been checked to make sure that the information is accurate and on topic.

If you find additional helpful resources on your own, be sure to Message me so that I can check them out and have them added to these resources that we all share! It's a good idea to print out the Cheat Sheets and keep them in a binder for handy reference. You can even add your own notes!
