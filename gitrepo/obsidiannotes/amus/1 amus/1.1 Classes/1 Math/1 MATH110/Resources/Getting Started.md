# Get Started!

Welcome to the course!  This page will guide you through how to access your course material, stay up-to-date on course news, and communicate with your instructor.  You can also access the **Syllabus** in the navigation bar at the top of your screen by first clicking on **Course Tools**. 

---

## Course Homepage

### Announcements & Updates

**Course Updates** and **Announcements** from your professor are shown first so that you can quickly keep up with "the news" of your course.  Once you have read it you can **dismiss** the message to hide it.

### Weekly Table of Contents

You will access the course content using the **Table of Contents tile icons** found prominently on your screen. Click on any week and you will be directed to all related learning material, activities, and assessments for the week. Checklists and progress bars will show your completion of the activities.

---

## Homepage Sidebar

### Instructor Information

Information about your instructor and **how to get in contact with them** are shown in the sidebar. Remember, you should be using your MyCampus email address to contact your instructors.

### Your MyCampus Email from Office 365

Quickly **access your MyCampus email account** through the Office 365 widget located in the sidebar. Once you login once, you will be logged in for all courses and spaces of MyClassroom. 

_If you do not know your MyCampus email address_ to login to the widget, go to the navigation bar at the top of this page, click on your name, and then “Notifications”. Your full email address will be displayed under “Contact Methods”. Use this in conjunction with your campus password to login to your MyCampus Email account, or the Office 365 widget.

### Reminders & Shortcuts

You will also find areas where you can see upcoming due dates on the calendar, as well as links to important university services such as the library, course materials, and classroom support.

---

## Top Navigation Bar

### Discussions & Lesson Content

Commonly accessed areas such as the weekly learning **content** as well as the **discussion** area are linked prominently in the top navigation bar for your convenience.

### Grades, Class List, & Help

The **Course Tools** menu contains links to the syllabus, your course grades, the list of students in the course, as well as other common areas in the classroom.

### Course Home

You can access this course's home page by clicking the title of the course, or by using the **Course Home** link.
