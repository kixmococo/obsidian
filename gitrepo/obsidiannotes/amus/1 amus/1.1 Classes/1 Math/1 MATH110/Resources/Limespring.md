## LimeSpring Lesson

Lessons are intended to introduce to you the material in the course, give you practice using the concepts introduced in that material, and assess your ability to do problems involving the material you've been learning and practicing. 

**LimeSpring** provides all the lesson content, e.g. videos, for each week of your course.  Embedded in these videos are quick, graded knowledge checks that count as your homework grades.  Before you can do your Workbook practice problems or take the Book exams which are also in **LimeSpring**, you must complete the corresponding videos and knowledge checks.   In addition to the **LimeSpring** videos that present your lesson content, if you have question about a topic covered in a video there are several places you can get help!

1. There are links in pdf files in the “Foundational Knowledge” at the top of your course’s content that take you to the **OpenStax** textbook on algebra.  Just look for the topic you have questions about, click the link to that topic and read more about it in the **OpenStax** textbook.
2. There are cheat sheets and handy helpers for every lesson that offer additional help on all topics.
3. You can use a tutor if you want to, for free!  The link for help from a tutor is at the bottom of every “Additional Resources” page in your course.

 Remember that the first person you should think of if you need help is your Instructor.  You can click on the “Envelope Icon” at the top of your browser page to Instant Message or email your Instructor directly with questions.  In addition, there is a Q&A Discussion in every week of your course. That discussion every week is for any questions you have.  If you have a question others probably will too!  So, check there to see if your question has already been posted and answered! In summary, go to **LimeSpring** to do all your work in your course, reading/videos about lesson topics, knowledge checks for homework grades, Workbooks for practice problems, and Book Exams.  The link to **LimeSpring** is in the Activities & Assessments block of every week.   Always remember you are not alone.  There are lots of places to get help any time you need it.  Good Luck!

## Lime Spring Modules

### There is one module for each week of class.

  
Click on the LimeSpring page under the Activities & Assessment section to access the link to LimeSpring. 

  
This is where you will complete your lessons, homework, workbooks and weekly tests.  Your homework score comes from the pop up quizzes during and at the end of each video.

  
Your goal is to earn five stars on every lesson.  Grades are automatically transferred within 24 hours.
