# **Overview:**

Welcome to College Algebra!

In this class we will review some of the algebra that you have learned before and introduce you to new concepts that will help you in future STEM classes - and in life!

Many of the most important lessons that you learn in math class - paying attention to detail, checking your work, making sure that you are answering the question that was asked, and creative problem solving - are lessons that will help you in many other aspects of your life.

Be sure to ask if you have questions about how to navigate the online resources or about the math! My focus is on helping you to understand this material - it's easier for me to do that if I know what's puzzling you!

I hope to help you brush up on things that you've forgotten, learn new math techniques, and prepare a strong foundation for future classes involving mathematics. I look forward to working with each of you to make this a successful semester.

## **Course Objective(s):**

CO2: Solve algebraic problems using equations.

CO3: Translate equations and inequalities into equivalent forms through the application of the rules of algebra.

CO9: Apply mathematical problem solving methods.

CO10: Develop mathematical models using one or two linear equations to solve an applications problem.

C011: Apply math concepts to situations in everyday life.
