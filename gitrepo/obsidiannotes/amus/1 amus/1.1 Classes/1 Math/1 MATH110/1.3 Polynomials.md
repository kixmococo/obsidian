Degree of a polynomial
	$x^2$ (2nd degree)
	$x^3$ (3rd degree)
		$xy^3$ (4th degree)
	etc

Ordering the terms in a polynomial
	1.group the terms alphabetically
	2.arrange the terms based on degree
$$\begin{split}
\text{example:}\\
&		x+y+x^2+3\\
&			x+x^2+y+3\\
&				x^2 +x +y +3\\
&		\text{(sort; alpha > degree)}
\end{split}$$
